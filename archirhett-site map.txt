Porftolio
These are four projects done in my later years of architectural school. These projects deal with community development, interdisplinary design, sustainable solutions, computer aided and comprehensive design.

Skin Prototype
Zoning in on the City Sense Competition entry, this Skin Prototype is an extension to the real time flux as well as responsive facade at the building envelope.

Urban Monastery
The comprehensive design studio project for an Urban Monastery and soup Kitchen located on the St. Louis Arch Greenway. the occupants of the building are a private community Benedictine Monks that would provide daily services.

Urban Intervetion
The Local Adaptabel Community Engagement Spaces project proposed to connect the disenfranchised community of west Baltimore. Bringing meaning, knowledge, and information accessible to any inhabitant.

Redline Competition
Undergraduate project competittion for an urban intervetion located in Baltimore. This urban equality hydroponic structure rose awareness and understanding of the future positive changes and implications that would follow to the community of West Baltimore.

City Sense Competition
the Atchafalay Basin was site location for the proposal for the City Sense Competition. The modular rural data collection mobile would utilize the flux of real-time data to help shape future growth and development.

Main Stree Workshop
Fourth year community development project focused on the Main Street in the small historic town of Minden, Louisiana. This project was exibited to Minden town hall meeting, where the designes provoked downtown rejuvenation and historical awareness.

Pedestrian Intervention
Third year project to repurpose an under utilized parking lot on a pedestrain walking campus. Program and intent was determined by site and context analysis which resulted in a sustainable multipurpose multimodal gathering and engaging transitional node.

Crosby Arboretum
Third year projects located within the Crosby Arboretum in the small town of Picayune, Mississippi. the first project is an Interpretive Center to showcase a selective sesitive econe for the unsuspecting visitors.

Ronchamp Mockup
Thirdyear project to erect a place of sacred architecture mockup to scale and tectonically accurate. The Ronchamp mockup of the Notre Dame du Haut designed by Le Corbusier took extensive research into vernacular tectonics and historical building tecnologies of locale materails and trades.

Rothko Museum
Second year Rothko Museum project set in Houston adjacent to the Menil Collection in a heavily residential neighborhood setting with extensively sensitive museums within walking distance.

Inanimate Object
First year project discovering structure lines for irregular object.

Atkinson Hall
First year project using hierarchy of structural and spatial components to reorient and display.

Continual Delay
Second year project incorporating anticipatorial anxiousness from a procedural path of continual delay to a view on an inconspicuous object.
